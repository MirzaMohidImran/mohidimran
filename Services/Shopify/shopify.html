Comprehensive Shopify Development Solutions

You need more than a Shopify store — you need a revenue machine. Complete Shopify Store Setup and Configuration

From Liquid theme development and custom app integrations to international multi-currency setups and Shopify Plus enterprise customizations — every store I build is fast (sub-2s load times), mobile-first, SEO-optimized, and built to grow. Whether you're launching a new brand, migrating from WooCommerce or Magento, or scaling an existing store past $1M in revenue — I have the technical depth and business understanding to make it happen.

Custom Shopify Theme & Liquid Development

Your brand deserves a unique digital storefront. I develop fully custom Shopify themes using Liquid, HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript that perfectly represent your brand identity. This includes responsive layouts, custom sections, reusable components, and advanced functionality tailored to your business requirements. All themes are lightweight, performance-optimized, and built to convert visitors into customers while maintaining exceptional page speed scores.

Shopify Plus Development for Enterprise

For rapidly scaling businesses, I offer comprehensive Shopify Plus development services that unlock enterprise-level capabilities. This includes advanced theme customization, Shopify Scripts implementation, custom checkout experiences, wholesale channel setup, multi-store management, automation workflows, and sophisticated integrations with your existing business systems. Perfect for high-volume merchants requiring advanced functionality and dedicated infrastructure.

Platform Migration to Shopify (WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce)

I provide secure and efficient platform migration services for businesses moving from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix, or custom ecommerce systems to Shopify or Shopify Plus. This includes complete data migration (products, customers, orders), URL structure preservation, 301 redirect implementation, SEO value retention, and thorough testing to ensure zero downtime and no data loss during the transition process.

Strategic Shopify App Integration

I integrate essential Shopify apps and third-party services that enhance your store's functionality and streamline operations. This includes payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net), shipping solutions, inventory management systems, email marketing platforms, customer support tools, analytics services, and custom API integrations. Each integration is carefully implemented to work seamlessly with your existing workflow.

Store Performance Optimization for Higher Conversions

Store speed directly impacts your conversion rate and search rankings. I optimize Shopify stores for lightning-fast loading times through strategic performance enhancements including image optimization, code minification, lazy loading implementation, third-party script optimization, and mobile performance tuning. This results in improved user experience, reduced bounce rates, and higher conversion rates across all devices.

Technical SEO for Shopify — Rank Higher, Sell More

All Shopify stores are built with comprehensive SEO best practices including semantic HTML structure, optimized meta tags, schema markup implementation, XML sitemap configuration, robots.txt optimization, canonical URL setup, and technical SEO auditing. My goal is to help your store rank higher in search engines and attract consistent, qualified organic traffic that converts into sales.

Marketing Automation & Revenue-Driving Integrations

I implement marketing automation solutions to streamline your customer engagement including abandoned cart recovery, email marketing workflows, customer segmentation, loyalty program setup, and integration with platforms like Klaviyo, Mailchimp, and HubSpot. These automated systems help you nurture leads, increase customer lifetime value, and maximize revenue without constant manual intervention.

Ongoing Shopify Support, Maintenance & Growth

You'll have dedicated support to address issues quickly and ensure your store operates at peak performance year-round.

frequently asked questions

  • What does a professional Shopify store development cost?
    Custom Shopify store development ranges from $800 for a basic branded store to $8,000+ for a fully custom Shopify Plus build with advanced integrations. Basic store setup: $800–$1,500. Custom theme development: $1,500–$4,000. Shopify Plus enterprise: $4,000–$8,000+. Full ecommerce solution with marketing automation: $3,000–$6,000. Pricing is value-based and transparent — you pay for business outcomes, not hours.
  • Can you handle Shopify development for businesses in USA, Dubai, and UK?
    Absolutely. I work with clients across the USA (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Miami, San Francisco), UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah), UK (London, Manchester, Birmingham), Canada (Toronto, Vancouver), Australia (Sydney, Melbourne), and 30+ other countries. I accommodate all time zones and can schedule calls during your business hours.
  • Do you develop for Shopify Plus enterprise businesses?
    Yes. Shopify Plus development includes custom checkout experiences with Checkout Extensibility, advanced Shopify Flow automation, Shopify Scripts for complex discounting, multi-store management, B2B wholesale portals, headless commerce with Hydrogen, and Shopify Markets for international selling. I build enterprise Shopify solutions that scale beyond 6-figure monthly revenue.
  • Can you migrate my WooCommerce or Magento store to Shopify?
    Yes, I provide complete platform migrations from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, OpenCart, and custom ecommerce systems to Shopify or Shopify Plus. Migration includes full product/customer/order data transfer, URL structure preservation with 301 redirects to protect SEO, theme recreation or custom design, payment gateway setup, and zero-downtime cutover.
  • How do you optimize Shopify stores for search engines (SEO)?
    Shopify SEO optimization includes: technical audit and fixes, schema markup (Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList), meta title/description optimization, URL structure cleanup, image alt text optimization, site speed improvement for Core Web Vitals, internal linking strategy, blog content setup, hreflang for international stores, and Google Search Console integration. I build stores that rank, not just look good.
