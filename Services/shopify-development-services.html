Shopify Development Services: Questions Answered

  • What are Shopify development services?
    Shopify development services cover everything needed to build, optimise, and scale a Shopify store. This includes custom theme development using Liquid, store setup and configuration, app integrations, Shopify Plus implementation, speed optimisation, technical SEO, migration from platforms like WooCommerce or Magento, and ongoing maintenance. A Shopify development expert handles both the technical build and the conversion strategy to ensure the store generates revenue, not just traffic.
  • How much does Shopify development cost?
    Shopify development costs vary based on scope. A basic custom store setup starts from around $500-$1,200. A full custom theme build or major redesign typically ranges from $1,500-$3,500. Shopify Plus projects, headless builds, or complex migrations can range from $3,500 to $6,000 and above. The investment depends on the number of custom templates, app integrations, design complexity, and whether ongoing SEO or CRO work is included.
  • What is the difference between Shopify and Shopify Plus?
    Shopify is the standard platform suited for most small to mid-sized ecommerce brands. Shopify Plus is an enterprise-grade plan designed for high-volume merchants processing over $1M in annual revenue. It offers advanced checkout extensibility, Shopify Flow automation, B2B and wholesale channels, Shopify Markets for international selling, dedicated support, and higher API rate limits.
  • How long does it take to build a Shopify store?
    A standard custom Shopify store build typically takes 2-4 weeks depending on the number of pages, design complexity, and required integrations. A Shopify redesign on an existing store can take 1-3 weeks. A full Shopify Plus implementation or platform migration may take 4-8 weeks, including QA, redirect planning, data migration, and post-launch testing.
  • Can you migrate my store from WooCommerce to Shopify?
    Yes. Shopify migration services cover full data migration including products, customers, orders, and blogs, plus 301 redirect mapping, technical SEO continuity, theme setup on Shopify, app stack replacement, and post-launch QA. Migrations from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix, and custom-built ecommerce platforms are all supported.
  • Do you offer Shopify SEO services?
    Yes. Shopify SEO services include a full technical audit, page template optimisation, collection and product page targeting, schema markup implementation, internal linking strategy, site speed improvements that directly impact Core Web Vitals, image optimisation, and content planning for collection pages.
  • What is headless Shopify development?
    Headless Shopify development decouples the storefront front-end presentation layer from the Shopify backend commerce engine. Instead of using Shopify's native Liquid theme system, a custom front-end is built using frameworks like Next.js or Hydrogen, connected to Shopify's Storefront API. This enables faster page loads, full design flexibility, and progressive web app functionality.
  • Do you work with brands in the USA, UK, UAE, and Australia?
    Yes. Shopify development services are available to brands across the USA, UK, UAE, Australia, Germany, France, Canada, and globally. All projects are delivered remotely with structured communication, milestone-based progress updates, and timezone-flexible scheduling.
