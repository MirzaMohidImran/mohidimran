frequently asked questions

  • What does a Shopify developer do?
    A Shopify developer builds, customizes, and optimizes Shopify stores. That can include theme development, custom sections, app integrations, migrations, performance work, technical SEO fixes, and ongoing store improvements.
  • Can you redesign my existing Shopify store without hurting SEO?
    Yes. A proper redesign should preserve or improve SEO by protecting key URLs, managing redirects carefully, keeping critical content mapped, and improving page structure rather than wiping it out.
  • Do you work on Shopify Plus?
    Yes. I support Shopify Plus work for brands that need more advanced storefront architecture, international expansion support, checkout extensibility planning, and growth-stage technical execution.
  • Can you migrate my store to Shopify?
    Yes. I handle migrations from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, and custom ecommerce systems with a focus on launch readiness, redirect planning, and continuity for rankings and revenue.
