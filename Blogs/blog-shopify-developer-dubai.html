Shopify for UAE Businesses: How to Set Up Your Store for the Dubai Market in 2026

The UAE e-commerce market is growing at 25% annually. Here's exactly how to set up Shopify to capture Dubai and GCC buyers with Arabic support, local payments, and UAE SEO.

The UAE consumer expects a premium, localized shopping experience. An English-only Shopify store with USD pricing targeting Dubai customers converts at a fraction of what an Arabic-enabled, AED-priced, locally-relevant store achieves. Localization is not optional — it's the difference between a store that sells and one that doesn't.
Mohid Imran

The UAE E-Commerce Opportunity in 2026

The UAE's e-commerce market reached $10 billion in 2025 and is projected to hit $17 billion by 2027. Dubai alone accounts for 45% of UAE e-commerce volume. The market is characterized by: high smartphone penetration (over 96%), affluent consumers willing to pay premium prices, strong preference for international brands, and growing comfort with online shopping post-pandemic. For Shopify merchants targeting UAE and GCC, the opportunity is enormous — if you set up your store correctly for the local market.

UAE Shopify Setup Checklist:

Arabic Language Support

RTL Arabic interface — increases UAE conversion rate by 40–60%.

AED Currency + UAE Payment Gateways

Telr, PayTabs, and Tabby/Tamara (BNPL) for UAE buyers.

UAE VAT Compliance

5% VAT registration and display requirements for UAE-based businesses.

Step 1: Enable Arabic and RTL Support

Shopify supports multiple languages natively through the Language editor. For Arabic, you need more than translation — Arabic is a right-to-left language requiring RTL CSS. The best approach: use Shopify's built-in translation with Weglot or Langify for Arabic product content, add dir="rtl" and lang="ar" to your Arabic pages, use Arabic-optimized fonts (Tajawal, Cairo, Noto Kufi Arabic), and test thoroughly on mobile where RTL layouts frequently break. A fully RTL Arabic Shopify store typically sees 40–60% higher conversion from Arabic-speaking UAE visitors.

Step 2: Configure UAE Payment Gateways

UAE consumers have specific payment preferences. Shopify Payments is not available in UAE — you need a third-party gateway. Top UAE payment gateways for Shopify: Telr (most popular, supports all UAE cards and Apple Pay), PayTabs (excellent for SMEs, easy setup), Network International (preferred for enterprise UAE businesses), and Checkout.com (global gateway with strong UAE support). BNPL is massive in UAE — integrate Tabby and Tamara as payment options. UAE shoppers using BNPL have 25–35% higher average order values.

Step 3: UAE VAT Setup and Compliance

Businesses with annual UAE revenue above AED 375,000 must register for UAE VAT (5%). For Shopify stores: enable tax collection in Shopify admin, configure the 5% UAE VAT rate for UAE shipping addresses, display prices inclusive or exclusive of VAT (UAE consumers expect VAT-inclusive pricing in most B2C contexts), generate VAT-compliant tax invoices for every order, and file quarterly VAT returns with the FTA. Use the Avalara Tax or TaxJar app for automated UAE VAT calculation.

Step 4: Local Logistics and Delivery

UAE consumers expect fast delivery — same-day delivery is common in Dubai for orders placed before noon. Integrate: Aramex (most used in UAE for cross-emirate shipping), FedEx UAE, DHL Express UAE, Fetchr (UAE-born delivery app with COD option), and Noon Express (if selling through Noon marketplace alongside Shopify). Cash on Delivery (COD) remains popular in UAE — 35–40% of UAE online orders are still paid via COD. Enable COD as a payment option with Shopify's native COD module.

  • Add WhatsApp Business chat button — primary customer service channel in UAE
  • Display your UAE phone number and address prominently — builds crucial trust
  • Use UAE-specific imagery and lifestyle photography that resonates with Gulf consumers
  • Optimize for Google UAE (.ae domain strategy) and Arabic keyword targeting
