How Much Does Shopify Development Cost in 2026? Complete Pricing Guide

Shopify development costs vary widely from $800 to $30,000+. This complete pricing guide explains what drives the cost and what you should expect to pay.

The cheapest Shopify developer is rarely the best investment. What looks like a $500 saving often costs $3,000 to fix six months later. Price your Shopify development based on the value of the business outcome, not the lowest hourly rate you can find.
Mohid Imran

The Honest Answer: It Depends on What You're Building

Shopify development costs in 2026 range from $500 for a basic store setup on an existing theme to $50,000+ for a fully custom Shopify Plus enterprise build with advanced integrations. That's not a cop-out — it genuinely depends on what you need. This guide breaks down every cost component so you can accurately budget your Shopify project and understand exactly what you're paying for at each price point.

Shopify Development Cost Tiers:

Tier 1: Basic Store Setup

$800–$1,500 — Premium theme installation, product upload, basic customization.

Tier 2: Custom Branded Store

$2,500–$5,000 — Custom theme design, brand-aligned development, full app integrations.

Tier 3: Shopify Plus Enterprise

$5,000–$20,000+ — Full custom build, checkout customization, B2B, automation.

Tier 1: Basic Shopify Store ($800–$1,500)

This covers: purchase and installation of a premium theme ($180–$350), basic theme customization (colors, fonts, logo), product catalog setup (up to 50 products), payment gateway configuration, shipping zone setup, basic SEO (meta tags, sitemap), and launch. This is appropriate for: small businesses launching their first online store, brick-and-mortar shops going digital, service businesses adding an online component. What it doesn't include: custom design, unique functionality, or anything that requires Liquid coding.

Tier 2: Custom Branded Shopify Store ($2,500–$5,000)

This is where most growing e-commerce brands sit. Includes: custom theme developed from scratch or heavily customized from a starter theme, custom Liquid section development for unique page layouts, brand-aligned design executed in code, app integrations (review apps, email marketing, loyalty, live chat), SEO optimization including schema markup and sitemap, mobile optimization audit, and performance tuning. Timeline: 3–5 weeks. Best for: DTC brands that need to stand out from the crowd.

Tier 3: Shopify Plus Enterprise ($5,000–$20,000+)

Shopify Plus ($2,000/month subscription) unlocks enterprise features that require developer expertise to implement. Development cost covers: Checkout Extensibility customization (scripts deprecated — new APIs required), Shopify Flow automation workflows, B2B wholesale portal development, multi-store/multi-language setup with Shopify Markets, headless frontend with Shopify Hydrogen, custom app development, and complex third-party integrations (ERP, PIM, OMS). Enterprises doing $500K+/month in revenue typically see ROI within 1–2 months.

Ongoing Shopify Costs to Budget For

  • Shopify subscription: $39/month (Basic) to $399/month (Advanced) to $2,000+/month (Plus)
  • Apps: $50–$500/month typical for a well-equipped store (reviews, email, returns, analytics)
  • Maintenance and updates: $200–$600/month for an ongoing retainer
  • Shopify transaction fees: 0.5–2% if not using Shopify Payments

Agency vs Freelancer: Which Is Better Value?

Shopify agencies charge $150–$250/hour and add project management overhead and account manager markup. A specialist freelancer charges $50–$120/hour with direct communication and lower overhead. For most Shopify projects under $15,000, a specialist freelancer delivers equivalent or better quality at 40–60% lower cost. For enterprise projects requiring large teams working simultaneously, an agency may be justified. My pricing sits at the premium end of freelancer rates — because the output quality reflects it.
